## Prioritized blockers

1. **P0 — The contribution hierarchy does not match the intended paper.** The title, abstract, contribution list, and full standalone DRH section foreground calibration-free planning (`main.tex:22,48–51,127–145,593–697`). Prefix-frontier entry occupies only `474–487`, has no algorithm or coverage proposition, and is absent as a named contribution. Rebuild the narrative around (i) an entry frontier that avoids global minimal-superset reduction, (ii) certified coarse blocks with level-local search, and (iii) construction of the resulting irregular graphs. Keep DRH as a secondary instantiation heuristic, with its negative results. Do not replace “calibration-free” with an unproved claim that the whole system eliminates LNG.

2. **P0 — Entry/frontier coverage and ANN quality are not yet separated rigorously.** `main.tex:570–572` says eligible points inside an unauthorized block “remain reachable” through another layer or the base. Authorization proves only eligibility of authorized direct members. It does not prove reachability of every eligible vector, nor bounded-search Recall. Add a separate frontier theorem at the group-topology level, then explicitly state the extra assumptions required to lift it to vector reachability. The current one-way safety proof and level-isolation proof can remain, but should not carry the coverage claim.

3. **P0 — Essential evidence for an entry-centered paper is missing from the rendered results.** `main.tex:955–956` promises an original-LNG/optimized-LNG/Trie compatibility ablation, but the supplied result macros contain no such table. Across-base factorials change both topology and entry (`generated_topology_factorial.tex:4,26`), so their gains cannot isolate frontier entry. `main.tex:962–969` nevertheless says Plain and *every* overlay share entry and all reported multilevel speedups isolate navigation scale; restrict this statement to the fixed-LNG-base controls. Report provider latency, output group count, downstream entry setup, Recall, and graph work under fixed compatible topology, and distinguish these from matched-provider system comparisons. Existing profiles already motivate the tradeoff: at 9.907%, 0L-Trie reduces ELS from 36.631 to 3.778 ms but increases entry setup from 0.646 to 14.744 ms and graph work from 98.747 to 323.081 ms (`generated_results.tex:226–227`). Faster discovery alone does not establish faster search.

4. **P0 — GPU headline is not supported by a visible quality result for its winning configuration.** The fastest composition uses `hybrid_gpu_intra` at 141.27 s / 1.35× (`generated_results.tex:303`), but the stated quality cohort names CPU, full-GPU, and full-GPU-WMMA sidecars, omitting that winner (`main.tex:883–885`). No query-quality comparison for accelerated builds appears in the supplied generated results. Also, adding GPU inter-block work slows the hybrid sidecar from 90.49 to 132.94 s, while WMMA takes 1011.13 s against 1000.64 s CPU (`generated_results.tex:282–286`). Preserve the useful construction contribution, but distinguish irregular intra-block acceleration from the fused cross-block kernel claim. Include quality for the winning composition and measured naive-SGEMM versus fused results before claiming that fusion explains the headline gain. The listed base profile definitions (`main.tex:754–776`) are not performance evidence.

5. **P1 — Closest-work distinction is too late and too generic.** UNG is explained repeatedly (`main.tex:66–74,177–195,1101–1112`), while Curator appears in one late paragraph (`1114–1125`). Add an early comparison covering the navigation unit, entry output contract, block membership/certification, query traversal, and construction mechanism. The defensible UNG delta is not “uses a Trie”: it is a branch-covering terminal frontier without global minimal-superset reduction, plus certified block overlays and level-local states. The draft describes Curator as a dual index sharing a clustering tree; explain precisely why that differs from canonical label-prefix certificates and same-level adjacency. Check that comparison against Curator before publishing it. “Complementary” alone does not establish novelty, and no direct performance claim against Curator is supported here.

## Required theorem contracts and wording repairs

- **Define frontier precisely.** Fix one total label order and unique sorted labels. Define `F(Q)` as the eligible observed terminals with no eligible proper terminal ancestor in the canonical Trie. For every eligible terminal `g`, its first eligible terminal ancestor is in `F(Q)`, and nearest-terminal-descendant edges form a path from that ancestor to `g`. All terminals on this path remain eligible. Thus seeding every member of `F(Q)` covers eligible *groups* without global minimal-superset reduction. Include `Q=empty` and an observed empty-label group. Outputs must be actual group IDs consumed by search.
- **Use a minimal counterexample.** With label order `a < b`, groups `{b}` and `{a,b}`, and query `{b}`, LNG minimal-superset reduction retains only `{b}`. The prefix frontier must retain both terminals because they lie on different prefix branches. This explains both the novelty and why reducing the frontier again would break coverage.
- **State vector assumptions separately.** Group coverage lifts to vector reachability only if each seeded group can reach every needed within-group vector and every materialized group transition has an accessible cross edge. It also requires retaining every frontier seed and exhaustive traversal for an exhaustive guarantee. A bounded queue may drop seeds or candidates. Independent visited sets protect `(vector, level)` identity, while final answers need deduplication by vector ID before selecting `K` (`main.tex:515–552`). No Recall monotonicity follows from level isolation.
- **Correct “Trie is a strict sparsifier.”** It is not necessarily an edge subset of the LNG transitive reduction (`main.tex:427–434`). For observed `{a}`, `{a,c}`, `{a,b,c}` with `a<b<c`, Trie has `{a}->{a,b,c}`, but LNG does not because `{a,c}` is an intermediate observed set. Both are containment-directed; Trie represents fewer containment reachability relations. Use “sparse prefix topology” unless the exact sparsifier relation is defined.
- **Call overlay membership a disjoint partial cover.** Algorithm 1 emits only non-root nodes with uncovered mass greater than `T` (`main.tex:384–419`). Residual groups can remain outside every emitted block. Each represented point has at most one direct owner per layer, but coverage of all points is not guaranteed. This matters to `main.tex:500` (“partition each layer”) and the safety argument. Also, `T` is an emission threshold, not an upper bound on block size; `N/T` is a structural proxy, not an exact block count.
- **Keep fallback equivalence narrow.** Its deterministic, same-provider, same-input, post-routing conditions are stated well (`main.tex:584–590`). They do not establish latency equivalence or that a Trie base matches an LNG base. Handle the no-overlay case explicitly before taking `max(level)` in Algorithm 3.

## Evidence consistency repairs before approval

- **Do not transfer factorial winners to the DRH headline.** The 14.40–26.70× abstract range belongs to LNG-base DRH-v1 (`generated_results.tex:62–72`), while the large Trie-base factorial gains use matched Trie entry and ungated routing. Both can be reported, but identify the actual configuration beside each claim. DRH-v2 has only separate held-out rows here.
- **Retain negative results prominently.** DRH-v1 is 0.205× Plain on VariousImg and DRH-v2 is 0.336× (`generated_results.tex:142,180`). DRH-v1 is also NC at Amazon 30% (`68`). Prefix-frontier coverage does not erase these finite-budget/search-policy failures. All-LNG-free superiority is unsupported; the factorial actually favors LNG at fine overlay levels in several workloads (`generated_topology_factorial.tex:51`).
- **Avoid inconsistent uses of NC.** The metric table defines NC as no Recall crossing (`main.tex:1013`); the profile uses NC for a timeout at 95% where a 0L-Trie crossing exists (`generated_results.tex:210,242`, factorial `18`). Use `timeout` for the profiling failure.
- **Correct batch-size scope.** `main.tex:991` states `B=1000` globally, but held-out batches contain 3000 or 2864 queries (`generated_results.tex:15–19`). Define `B` per workload.
- **Make reported protocols self-contained.** Query results have two warm repeats; construction also has two measured repeats despite describing a five-repeat target (`main.tex:1021–1034`). Bootstrap intervals from this very small construction sample should be labeled exploratory, and no unexecuted formal pass should read as completed evidence. Hardware/CPU/GPU specifications are missing from the supplied main text. State observed setup and evidence limits once; move deadline/manifest/recovery vocabulary out of the scientific argument.
- **Align construction cohorts.** The preliminary base numbers 190.61/53.18 s (`generated_results.tex:270`) differ from the repeated composition's 190.38 s baseline and approximately 50.78 s accelerated base (`300–304`). Identify distinct cohorts or use the authoritative repeated values consistently. Continue labeling 1.35× as a sum of independently measured stage medians, not a jointly timed build.

**Approval condition:** the reorganized draft must make the frontier contract and conditional proof explicit, repair the coverage and attribution overstatements, and either supply the missing provider/build-quality evidence or narrow the affected claims. Merely moving current paragraphs will not resolve those blockers.
