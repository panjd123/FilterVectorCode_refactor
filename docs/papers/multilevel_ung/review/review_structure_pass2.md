- Proposition 1 is a valid **label-plane** coverage argument (`sections/frontier.tex:25–41`). The requirements for lifting it to vector reachability are separate, and finite-budget Recall is explicitly experimental.

Its induction supports eligibility of computed distances under these conditions. The partial-cover qualification, distinct `(vector, level)` state, answer deduplication, retagging, and fallback boundary are all meaningful improvements.

- Curator, ACORN, and other related work are represented with more precise scope. The draft no longer builds its novelty on a blanket claim that other systems cannot handle conjunctions or low selectivity. Build-time gains are separated from unestablished quality equivalence.

## Experimental blockers, not solvable by rewriting

- **Entry contribution attribution and transfer.** The paper now admits that the current results are not a controlled three-provider ablation (`evaluation.tex:31–33`). For a frontier-centered systems contribution, measure discovery time, candidate/frontier sizes, ancestor/minimality work, seed setup, and downstream Recall under controlled conditions. Because the measured prefix provider enumerates all eligible groups whereas the LNG providers first collect a prefix frontier, complexity alone cannot establish its practical advantage. Additional datasets should test the prefix-plus-block design itself; the current held-out results primarily test the conservative LNG-base DRH policy.
- **Current-protocol external baselines.** Plain is a modified, optimized UNG control, and the factorial establishes internal design effects. It does not establish competitiveness with current UNG/ACORN/Curator or other suitable baselines. The literature audit improves positioning but cannot substitute for these measurements. The draft correctly says so at `discussion_related.tex:17–20`.
- **Construction quality and mechanism.** Query-quality checks for the fastest hybrid composition and other accelerated indexes remain necessary before treating build-time ratios as equivalent-quality acceleration. The current timing rows also show GPU inter-block work slowing the hybrid build and WMMA near CPU time (`generated_results.tex:282–286`). Report these failures and isolate the fused operation if fusion is to carry a distinct empirical contribution. The present qualified time-only report is honest but preliminary.
- **Robustness and resource evidence.** Two warm query repeats and two construction repeats support screening, not strong uncertainty estimates. A full systems evaluation also needs index size/loaded-memory comparisons across relevant topology and overlay configurations, and enough recall targets to show the result is not tied to one 0.90 crossing. Hardware reporting is pending separately and should be completed before reproducibility review.

## Overall judgment

The major pass-1 conceptual errors have been repaired: frontier coverage is not ANN completeness, certification is conditional on ownership, residual points are acknowledged, and sparse prefix edges are not equated with an LNG subgraph. I found no new contradiction in the two central proof arguments when read under their explicit assumptions. The next manuscript work should be targeted: align the results with the new narrative, specify query-state retention, and repair the remaining table identities. Stronger empirical claims should wait for the named experiments rather than be created through more assertive prose.
