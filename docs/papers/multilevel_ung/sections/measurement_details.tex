\subsection{Variation in the Recorded Warm Repeats}
Let $t_{A,1},t_{A,2}$ and $t_{B,1},t_{B,2}$ be the two warm batch times for
configurations $A$ and $B$ at their selected crossings. The reported QPS
ratio is $\operatorname{median}(t_B)/\operatorname{median}(t_A)$.
For equal batch sizes, the smallest and largest of the four observed
cross-repeat QPS ratios are
\[
 \left[\frac{\min(t_B)}{\max(t_A)},\;
       \frac{\max(t_B)}{\min(t_A)}\right].
\]
This descriptive range does not assume paired executions. Its separation
from one is not a significance test or evidence of stability across new
process runs. Reconstructing all 95 crossings from their original two warm
timings reproduces the frozen QPS and minimum Recall. Their sample
time coefficients of variation have median 0.87\% and maximum 27.03\%.
Table~\ref{tab:warm-repeat-ranges} compares adding L2 with replacing its
connectivity. The 95\% TLT/TLL point ratio is 1.251, but its observed range
is 1.004--1.502, illustrating why point rankings alone can overstate precision.

\subsection{Artifact Access}
The \href{https://github.com/panjd123/FilterVectorCode_refactor}{source repository}
contains the manuscript, experiment scripts, and frozen summary CSVs under
\path{docs/papers/multilevel_ung/generated_data/}. In particular,
\texttt{factorial\_equal\_recall.csv} records selected capacity, minimum
warm-repeat Recall, QPS, status, and method identifier for all 126 cells.
The experiment README gives both a source-only plotting command and the
existing-run finalization command. Full timing reproduction additionally
requires the vector, label, query, index, and raw-run files named by the
recorded configurations; these large inputs are not distributed in the
source repository.
The extracted warm timing pairs and their source hashes are included under
\path{generated_data/warm_repeats/}; a source-only script regenerates the
descriptive ranges without rerunning search.
The compact query records, exact label coverages, and size/selectivity
histograms are included under \path{generated_data/query_strata/}; they
reproduce the subgroup tables without the external vector files.
